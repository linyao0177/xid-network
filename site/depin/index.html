DePIN on xid

A network that rewards devices for sensing or serving somewhere (weather, air quality, energy) picks a template, changes what it needs and deploys. The xid network's compute nodes become its oracles: they score every epoch from what the devices signed, settle the rewards root on the registry and are paid for it. Devices claim with a Merkle proof.

Below: the testnet's own weather network, live. Sixteen simulated stations near the four testnet nodes; two of them claim places they are not.

One epoch

  1. Devices signReadings with a time, a join record naming their H3 cell and payee, beacons over the radio and the beacons they hear.
  2. Oracles collectThe network's assigned compute nodes follow every enrolled device and time a signed ping each minute.
  3. SheetsAfter the epoch each oracle publishes what it holds on each device, and an index of it signed by its stake key.
  4. ScoreFrom all oracles' sheets: a message counts when most hold it. Location by round trips, coverage by witnesses, crowding by area.
  5. SettleA quorum signing the same root settles it on the registry; anyone publishes it on the network, within the curve's emission.
  6. ClaimAnyone claims a device's reward for the payee it named, with a proof against the root.

Take part

Add a station

Simulated stations of this network wherever you say they are. The testnet pays their enrollment. Pick a home node near you; claim a place an oracle cannot reach that fast and the round trips rule it out.

npm install -g https://iot.xid.network/dl/node/xid-depin-0.1.0.tgz
xid-depin get ./wx
xid-depin sim ./wx --home <node key> --stations "home@25.03,121.56"

Node keys are on the testnet page and in testnet.json.

Enroll a device

A device of your own that publishes the readings, join record and pongs (the formats): paste its key and the testnet enrolls it.

Start a network

Weather, air quality or energy, as a template; your reward curve, your token, your rules. The network's oracles are the xid nodes, paid from a job budget your enrollment fees top up.

xid-depin templates
xid-depin new air-quality ./air --name my-air
xid-depin deploy ./air --testnet --key admin.key --mint
xid-depin keeper ./air --home <node key> --key admin.key --claim
Data from the testnet hub, pushed every 30 seconds. Anyone can score an epoch again from the oracles' published sheets: xid-depin audit.
